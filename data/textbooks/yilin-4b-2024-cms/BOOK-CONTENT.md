## Unit 1 We're friends

### Lead-in
- What can friends do for each other?	朋友能为彼此做什么？
- I share carrots with my friends.	我和朋友分享胡萝卜。
- Friends help each other.	朋友互相帮助。

### Words
- weak	虚弱的，无力的
- strong	强壮的
- there are	有
- them	它们；他们；她们
- from	来自
- party	聚会，联欢会
- us	我们
- friendship	友谊
- forest	森林
- thin	瘦的
- back	背部
- angry	发怒的，生气的
- one day	有朝一日
- how	怎样，如何
- Please!	求求你！
- Go away!	走开！
- net	网
- sad	悲哀的，难过的
- Help!	救命啊！
- there is	有
- hole	洞
- kind	友好的，体贴的
- become	变成，变为

### Cartoon Time
- Ruby: There are so many carrots. I want to share them with my friends.
- Bobby: There are some carrots.
- Bobby: They're from my friend Ruby.
- Bobby: Thank you for the carrots, Ruby. We want to share our fruit with you.
- Ruby: Thank you. Let's have a fruit party!
- Friends: Great!
- Ruby: The fruit is so sweet.
- Bobby: Like our friendship!

### Story Time
- Narrator: There are many animals in the forest. The lion is big and strong. The mouse is small and thin.
- Narrator: The mouse is on the lion's back. The lion is angry.
- Mouse: Please don't eat me. I can help you one day.
- Lion: Ha ha! How can you help me? You're so weak!
- Mouse: Please!
- Lion: OK. Go away!
- Narrator: The lion is in the net. He is sad.
- Lion: Help!
- Mouse: Let me help you.
- Narrator: There is a big hole in the net.
- Lion: Thank you! You're so kind!
- Narrator: The lion and the mouse become good friends.

## Unit 2 Helping others at school

### Lead-in
- How can you help others at school?	你在学校怎样帮助别人？
- I can show you around.	我可以带你参观。

### Words
- other	另外，其他
- room	房间
- computer	计算机，电脑
- swing	秋千
- playground	游戏场
- have a look	看一看
- careful	小心，谨慎
- feel	觉得，感到
- library	图书馆
- first	第一
- floor	楼层
- show around	带……参观
- any	任何的，任一的
- third	第三
- later	以后，后来
- piano	钢琴
- second	第二

### Cartoon Time
- Bobby: Sam, is there a swing in our new playground?
- Sam: Yes, there is. Let's go and have a look.
- Bobby: The swing is great!
- Sam: Let's go and play!
- Narrator: Now Bobby is on the swing.
- Bobby: Go! Go! Go!
- Sam: Be careful, Bobby.
- Sam: Are you OK?
- Bobby: Yes, but I feel dizzy ...
- Sam: It's time for Art. Let's go to the art room.
- Sam: Let me help you.
- Bobby: Thank you!

### Story Time
- Yang Ling: Here's your book.
- Nancy: Thanks. I want to take these books to the library.
- Nancy: I can help you. But where's the library? I'm new here.
- Yang Ling: It's on the first floor. Come with me.
- Yang Ling: I'm Yang Ling. Welcome.
- Nancy: I'm Nancy. Our school is so big.
- Yang Ling: I can show you around.
- Nancy: Thanks. You're so kind.
- Yang Ling: Let's go to the art room first. It's on the first floor too.
- Nancy: Great. Are there any computer rooms in our school?
- Yang Ling: Yes, there are. They're on the third floor. We can go there later.
- Yang Ling: The music room is on the third floor too.
- Nancy: Is there a piano?
- Yang Ling: Yes, there is.
- Nancy: Right. Thank you for showing me around.
- Yang Ling: Our classrooms are here on the second floor.
- Yang Ling: You're welcome.

## Unit 3 Road safety

### Lead-in
- How do you keep safe on the road?	你怎样在路上保持安全？
- Wait for the green traffic light.	等绿灯。

### Words
- road	道路，公路
- safety	安全，平安
- bus	公共汽车，巴士
- traffic light	交通信号灯
- take	乘坐，搭乘
- busy	人来车往的
- stay	保持
- close	接近，靠近
- cross	穿越，横过
- stop	停止，停下
- wait	等候，等待
- left	向左边
- right	向右边
- safe	安全的

### Cartoon Time
- Sister: Let's go to Aunt Alice's home.
- Bobby: Great idea! We can take a bus.
- Narrator: There are many cars and buses on the road.
- Bobby: Yes, it's busy.
- Sister: The traffic light is red now.
- Bobby: We can't go.
- Bobby: Look! There are ducks on the road.
- Bobby: We can't go again!
- Duck: Stay close! Don't run! Good little ducks.

### Story Time
- Haohao: Mum, let's cross the road.
- Mum: Stop, Haohao!
- Mum: The traffic light is red. We can't cross the road now. Wait for the green traffic light.
- Haohao: I'm sorry.
- Taotao: Mum, the traffic light is green. We can go now.
- Taotao: Wait. Look left and right first.
- Mum: That's right, Taotao.
- Haohao: It's safe now. We can cross the road.
- Haohao: That's our bus.
- Taotao: Haohao, don't run on the road! It's not safe.
- Haohao: OK.

## Unit 4 Caring about others

### Lead-in
- How do you take care of others?	你怎样照顾别人？
- What's the matter?	怎么了？

### Words
- care about	关心，关怀
- hungry	感到饿的
- thirsty	渴的，口渴的
- ill	不舒服，有病
- tired	疲倦的，疲劳的
- hot	觉得闷热
- water	水
- have a rest	休息一下
- What's the matter?	怎么了？
- home	到家，在家
- bag	包，袋
- bread	面包
- dear	亲爱的
- set the table	摆放餐具

### Cartoon Time
- Tom: I'm hot and thirsty, Bobby.
- Bobby: Here's some water for you, Tom.
- Tom: I'm hungry, Bobby.
- Bobby: Have a cake, Tom.
- Bobby: Tom, please be quiet. Let's have a rest.
- Tom: No, I want to play!
- Mum: What's the matter, Bobby?
- Bobby: I'm tired ...
- Tom: Can I come tomorrow?

### Story Time
- Su Hai: Hi, Mum! Let me take your coat.
- Su Yang: I can take your bag, Mum.
- Mum: Hi, I'm home!
- Su Yang: What's the matter, Mum?
- Mum: I'm tired.
- Su Yang: Have a rest, Mum.
- Su Hai: Are you hungry, Mum? Would you like some bread?
- Mum: No, thanks. I'm not hungry. I'm thirsty.
- Su Hai: Here's some water.
- Mum: Thank you, dear.
- Dad: It's six thirty. It's time for dinner!
- Su Yang: Great! Let's set the table, Su Hai.
- Su Hai: OK.

## Unit 5 Eating out

- What would you like?	你想要什么？

### Words
- eat out	去饭店吃饭
- fried rice	炒饭
- spring roll	春卷
- noodle	面条
- tea	茶
- juice	果汁
- menu	菜单
- What would you like?	你想要什么？
- fish and chips	炸鱼薯条
- sandwich	三明治
- hot dog	热狗
- delicious	美味的，可口的
- Anything else?	还要其他东西吗？
- a glass of	一杯
- a cup of	一杯
- food	食物，食品
- next time	下次
- hot pot	火锅
- takeaway box	打包盒

### Cartoon Time
- Bobby: Let's eat there.
- Waiter: Here's our menu. What would you like?
- Bobby: I'd like some fish and chips, please.
- Sam: A sandwich, please.
- Bobby: A hot dog?
- Sam: How's the hot dog?
- Bobby: It's delicious!
- Bobby: Thank you!

### Story Time
- Waiter: What would you like?
- Mike: I'd like some jiaozi.
- Wang Bing: I'd like some noodles.
- Dad: I'd like some fried rice and spring rolls.
- Wang Bing: I'd like some spring rolls too. We can share them.
- Waiter: Can I help you?
- Dad: Yes. We'd like noodles, jiaozi, fried rice and spring rolls, please.
- Waiter: OK. Anything else?
- Dad: Two glasses of orange juice and a cup of tea, please.
- Mike: It's delicious. I love Chinese food!
- Wang Bing: Great! Next time, let's try hot pot. It's Sichuan food.
- Dad: Two takeaway boxes, please!

## Unit 6 Jobs

### Lead-in
- What makes a job great?	什么让一份工作变得了不起？
- Different jobs help people in different ways.	不同的工作以不同方式帮助人们。

### Words
- job	工作，职业
- driver	驾驶员，司机
- nurse	护士
- worker	工人
- teacher	教师
- doctor	医生
- farmer	农民
- cook	厨师；烹饪
- police officer	警察
- hospital	医院
- parent	父亲，母亲
- work	工作
- sick	生病的，有病的
- people	人，人们
- busy	忙碌的
- taxi	出租汽车，的士
- train	火车
- place	地方
- way	方法，方式

### Cartoon Time
- Henry: Look at the hospital, Henry! What about your parents?
- Bobby: What are your parents' jobs, Bobby?
- Bobby: My dad is a doctor. My mum is a nurse. They help sick people.
- Henry: My dad is a driver. My mum is a worker. They're busy.
- Bobby: Is your dad a bus driver?
- Henry: No, he isn't.
- Henry: My dad is a train driver. Look!
- Bobby: Wow! That's cool!

### Story Time
- Wang Bing: What are your parents' jobs, Liu Tao?
- Liu Tao: My father is a taxi driver. Drivers take people to different places. My mother is a cook. Cooks make delicious food.
- Liu Tao: What's your father's job?
- Mike: Doctors help sick people.
- Liu Tao: What about your mother?
- Mike: She's a worker. She can make nice sweets.
- Liu Tao: I love sweets!
- Mike: What's your father's job, Yang Ling?
- Yang Ling: He's a police officer. Police officers keep us safe.
- Yang Ling: Teachers help children learn new things.
- Students: Our parents have great jobs. They help people in different ways.

## Unit 7 Doing chores at home

### Lead-in
- What chores can you do at home?	你在家能做什么家务？
- I can do chores at home.	我能在家做家务。

### Words
- chore	日常事务
- dish	碟，盘
- water	给……浇水
- make the bed	铺床
- what	什么
- house	房屋，房子
- Good job!	干得漂亮！
- part of	……的一部分
- so	所以
- half	一半，半
- past	晚于，过
- weekend	周末

### Cartoon Time
- Narrator: It's Sunday. Let's do some chores.
- Mum: Bobby, what are you doing?
- Bobby: I'm washing the dishes.
- Mum: What are you doing now?
- Bobby: I'm washing the clothes.
- Bobby: I'm watering the flowers.
- Mum: You're watering the floor!
- Mum: What a clean house!
- Mum: Good job!

### Story Time
- Narrator: It's Saturday. Mum and Dad are doing chores. Mum is cooking lunch. Dad is washing the clothes.
- Narrator: Su Yang and I are also doing chores. We're part of the family, so we want to help. Su Yang is watering the flowers. What am I doing? I'm making the bed.
- Narrator: It's half past twelve. It's after lunch. Mum is cleaning the table. Su Yang is washing the dishes. I'm feeding our cat.
- Narrator: Our home is clean and nice now. We have a busy weekend, but we all feel happy.

- him	他

- cut	切，割

### Cartoon Time
- Sam: I can cook fish soup for him!
- Sam: Happy Birthday, Dad!
- Dad: Thank you!
- Dad: Sam, are you cooking?
- Sam: Oh, my fish soup!
- Dad: Thank you! But be careful next time.

### Story Time
- Liu Tao: Are you cooking dinner, Mum?
- Mum: Yes, I am. I'm cutting the potatoes.
- Liu Tao: You're busy. Can I help?
- Mum: Yes. Wash the tomatoes, please.
- Liu Tao: OK, Mum.
- Liu Tao: Are you cooking meat, Dad?
- Dad: Yes. I'm cooking Kung Pao Chicken.
- Liu Tao: Great! I can't wait! Let me get a dish for you.
- Dad: Thank you, Taotao.
- Dad: How's the chicken, Taotao?
- Liu Tao: It's delicious, Dad. You're a great cook!
- Mum: How are the potatoes, Haohao?
- Haohao: They're nice. I love potatoes!
- Liu Tao: Have some tomato and egg soup, Dad and Mum.
- Parents: Thanks!
