Narrator: Yuan Longping was a Chinese rice scientist. He was born in 1930 in Beijing. He passed away in 2021 in Changsha.
Narrator: He did a lot of studies on rice. People call him the father of hybrid rice.
Yuan Longping: I have two dreams. I want to enjoy the cool under tall rice plants. I also want to see hybrid rice grow all over the world.
Narrator: Yuan liked farming at an early age. When he grew up, he studied farming. In 1964, he began to work on hybrid rice.
Narrator: His work was difficult, but he never stopped trying. Finally, he developed hybrid rice and helped many people.
