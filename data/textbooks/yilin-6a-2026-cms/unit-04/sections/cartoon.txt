Narrator: It is the Double Ninth Festival today.
Bobby: Do you know the Double Ninth Festival, Tina?
Tina: Yes, it's a day for old people. It's on the ninth day of the ninth lunar month.
Bobby: What do people do on this day?
Tina: They show love for their grandparents.
Bobby: We can cook noodles and make a card for Grandpa.
Narrator: Bobby, Tina and their parents go to see Grandpa in the afternoon.
Bobby and Tina: Happy Double Ninth Festival, Grandpa! We love you!
Grandpa: Thank you! I love you too!
Grandpa: My birthday is next Monday. I can't wait!
