Narrator: Long ago, there was a poor man. His name was Meng Xin. He had a sick old ox.
Narrator: One day, Meng Xin was away from home. His nephew sold the ox. Meng Xin came back home. He did not see the ox.
Meng Xin: Did you buy my ox?
Buyer: Yes, I did.
Meng Xin: Sorry, I can't sell it to you. It's old and sick.
Buyer: It's very kind of you. Thank you.
Narrator: Meng Xin got back his sick old ox. He got a good name too.
