# On Grandpa's farm
Liu Tao: Hello, Grandpa!
Mike: Hello, Grandpa!
Grandpa: Hello, boys! Welcome to my farm.
Liu Tao: What are these, Grandpa?
Grandpa: They're pigs.
Mike: Is this a home for pigs?
Grandpa: Yes, it is.
Mike: What are those under the tree? Are they sheep?
Grandpa: No, they aren't. They're cows.
Liu Tao: These rabbits are very cute.
Mike: Yes, they are. What do they like?
Grandpa: They like carrots.
Liu Tao: Are those chickens?
Grandpa: No, they aren't. They're ducks.
Liu Tao: One, two, three, four ... Don't move, ducks!
Liu Tao: Grandpa, your farm is fun.
