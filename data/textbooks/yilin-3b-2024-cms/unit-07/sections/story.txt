# At the zoo
Mum: Look at the tigers!
Liu Tao: I like tigers. They're cool.
Dad: I like tigers too.
Liu Tao: Do you like bears, Mum?
Mum: Yes, I do. They're big.
Dad: Do you like lions, Haohao?
Haohao: No, I don't! I like pandas!
Mum: Haohao, look at the monkeys. They're cute.
Haohao: I like monkeys! Hi, Monkey! Would you like a banana?
Narrator: Don't feed animals in the zoo!
Haohao: Oh, I'm sorry!
Mum: That's all right. Let's go to see the pandas.
