# A little friend
Teacher: Look, this is a chameleon. It's my little friend. What colour is it?
Girl: It's green.
Teacher: What colour is it now? Can you see it?
Girl: Yes, it's here.
Boy: It's brown.
Girl: It's yellow and red now.
Boy: It's cool!
Teacher: Yes, my little friend is very cool.
Teacher: What colour is it now?
Boy: It's green again!
Boy: How come?
Girl: Wow!
